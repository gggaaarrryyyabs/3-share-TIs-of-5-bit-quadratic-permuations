Optimal three-share TIs of Q_30^5 and Q_32^5
================================================================================

All additions and products are over F_2.
Bit convention: x = a + 2b + 4c + 8d + 16e.
Input shares: x^(i) = (a_i,b_i,c_i,d_i,e_i), i=1,2,3.

Here "optimal" means a one-stage uniform 3-share TI without fresh randomness,
with no changing of the guards.  Both S-boxes are quadratic, so 3 shares attain
the t+1 lower bound.

The correction maps below were found computationally.  Each resulting complete
15-bit shared map was independently exhaustively verified on all 2^15 inputs.
