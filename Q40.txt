Optimal TIs of Q_40^5 
====================================

All additions are over F_2.

For both S-boxes, use the bit convention
x = a + 2b + 4c + 8d + 16e,

and let the three input shares be
x^(i) = (a_i, b_i, c_i, d_i, e_i),  i = 1,2,3.

Each optimal TI is written as
Q^(i) = (A_i, B_i, C_i, D_i, E_i).
