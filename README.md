Božilov et. al. [https://doi.org/10.13154/tosc.v2017.i1.398-404] marked *unkown* for 3-share TIs of some 5-bit quadratic permutations without fresh randomness.
We provide 14 additional 3-share TIs of quadratic permutatoins to decrease the number of marked *unknown*.
All 5-bit quadratic permutations with known optimal TIs have differential uniformity greater than or equal 16.

PS: There may be other 5-bit quadratic permutations admitting 3-share TIs.
